Privacy Policy for Astral Sling

Application: Astral Sling
Package ID: com.radhika.astralsling

This Privacy Policy explains how Astral Sling ("the Game", "we", "our", or "us") handles user and device information across Android phones, tablets, and Android TV / Google TV devices. We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing complete transparency regarding our data practices.

1. Information We Collect Directly

We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information directly. Astral Sling does not require user accounts, logins, or social profile integration. Specifically, we do not collect or access:

  • Name, physical address, email address, or phone number
  • Contacts, photos, media files, microphone, or camera
  • Precise GPS location data

2. On-Device Local Storage

To save your high scores, maximum sector reached, and collected Star-Cells / in-game progress between gameplay sessions, the Game uses standard on-device web storage (localStorage). This gameplay progress remains strictly on your device and is never uploaded, synced, or transmitted to any external server managed by us.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

On supported mobile devices (phones and tablets), Astral Sling may integrate Google AdMob to display occasional interstitial ads or optional rewarded video advertisements (for example, to grant an optional second-chance revive). On Android TV and Google TV devices, advertising SDK requests are disabled.

When Google AdMob is active, Google and its certified ad partners may automatically collect and process certain device and usage telemetry to serve advertisements, combat fraudulent activity, and measure ad performance. This data may include:

  • Device Identifiers: Android Advertising ID (AAID) or app-set IDs.
  • Approximate Location: Coarse region inferred from IP address.
  • Device & Network Data: Device model, operating system version, carrier, and language settings.
  • Usage Metrics & Diagnostics: Ad impressions, clicks, user interactions, and performance/crash telemetry.

All third-party data processing is governed by Google’s respective policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google Uses Information from Sites or Apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Opting Out of Personalized Advertising:
Where required by applicable law (such as in the EEA/UK), ad personalization respects your consent choices. You can also opt out of personalized ads or reset/delete your Android Advertising ID at any time via your device settings:
Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads).

4. Device Permissions & Display Behavior

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Used to request and display optional third-party advertisements via Google AdMob on supported mobile devices, and to open external policy links when selected by you. Core arcade gameplay works offline.
  • Screen Keep-Awake (FLAG_KEEP_SCREEN_ON): Keeps the display active during gameplay sessions to prevent the screen from dimming while orbiting. No personal data is accessed or recorded.

5. Data Sharing, Retention, and Data Deletion

We do not operate backend servers, maintain user profile databases, or sell user information. Any data processed by Google AdMob is retained in accordance with Google’s data retention schedules.

Because all gameplay progress is saved locally on your device, you can permanently erase all stored game data at any time by clearing the application storage via:
Settings → Apps → Astral Sling → Storage → Clear Storage / Clear Data, or simply by uninstalling the application.

6. Children’s Privacy

Astral Sling does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). In accordance with Google Play’s Families Policy and the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), whenever the application or ad requests are configured for child-directed or mixed audiences, ad requests are tagged for child-directed treatment (TAG_FOR_CHILD_DIRECTED_TREATMENT) so that only contextual, non-personalized advertising is requested.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe personal data has been inadvertently collected, please contact us immediately so we can ensure proper safeguards or data removal.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates, new platform integrations, or legal compliance requirements. Any revisions will be published at this URL with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp.
